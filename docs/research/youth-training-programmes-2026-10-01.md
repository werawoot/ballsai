# Youth training programmes we may build on — 1 Oct 2026

Question: for the new **Training** feature (athletes pick a programme, set weekly days, check
in sessions, earn self-reported XP that never touches performance ratings; content shown as
"Draft — awaiting coach review" until a licensed coach signs off), which world-class
programmes can we reference, under what terms, and what does authoritative guidance say for
**U10–U13** and **U14–U17**?

## สรุปภาษาไทย (สำหรับเจ้าของ)

- **FIFA 11+ (อายุ 14 ปีขึ้นไป) และ 11+ Kids (7–13 ปี)** มีหลักฐานดีที่สุด: 11+ Kids ทำให้บาดเจ็บลดลง 48%
  และบาดเจ็บรุนแรงลดลง 74% (RCT 4 ประเทศ) ส่วน 11+ ลดการบาดเจ็บโดยรวมราว 39% (meta-analysis)
- **ข้อกำหนดการใช้งานของ FIFA ห้ามคัดลอกหรือนำเนื้อหาไปเผยแพร่** (วิดีโอ รูป โปสเตอร์ ข้อความ
  และชื่อ/โลโก้) นอกจากใช้ส่วนตัวที่ไม่ใช่เชิงพาณิชย์บนแพลตฟอร์มของ FIFA เอง → **ห้ามฝัง ห้ามก๊อป
  วิดีโอ/รูป/โปสเตอร์**
- เราอธิบาย "ท่าฝึก" ด้วยคำของเราเองเป็นภาษาไทยได้ เพราะกฎหมายลิขสิทธิ์ไทย มาตรา 6 ไม่คุ้มครอง
  "ขั้นตอน กรรมวิธี ระบบ วิธีใช้หรือทำงาน" แต่ **ต้องลิงก์กลับไปแหล่งเดิม** และ **ห้ามตั้งชื่อโปรแกรมว่า
  "FIFA 11+"** หรือทำให้ดูเหมือน FIFA รับรอง (ควรให้ฝ่ายกฎหมายหรือ FIFA ยืนยันก่อนเปิดใช้งานจริง)
- สมาคมฟุตบอลอังกฤษ, DFB, KNVB, US Soccer และ Football Australia **ห้ามนำเนื้อหาไปใช้ซ้ำในแอปเชิงพาณิชย์ทั้งหมด**
  ใช้ได้เพียงเป็น "หลักการ" อ้างอิง (เช่น Four Corner Model, กลุ่มอายุ, ฝึกเกมเล็ก) แล้วออกแบบดริลเอง
- แนวทางอายุ: หยุดพักอย่างน้อย 1–2 วันต่อสัปดาห์, ชั่วโมงฝึกต่อสัปดาห์ไม่เกินอายุ (ปี),
  พักจากฟุตบอลรวม 3 เดือนต่อปี (AAP) ส่วนเวทหรือบอดี้เวทเริ่มได้ราว 7–8 ปี สัปดาห์ละ 2–3 ครั้ง ครั้งละ 20–30 นาที
  ภายใต้การดูแลของผู้ฝึก
- ช่วงโตเร็ว (PHV: หญิงราว 11–13 ปี ชายราว 13–15 ปี) เสี่ยงบาดเจ็บที่ growth plate เช่น Osgood-Schlatter
  (ปวดใต้เข่า) และ Sever (ปวดส้นเท้า) แอปต้องมีปุ่ม "เจ็บ/ปวด" ที่หยุดโปรแกรมและให้ไปพบผู้ใหญ่หรือแพทย์
- ความร้อน: ใช้ระดับดัชนีความร้อนของกรมอนามัย (เฝ้าระวัง 27–32.9 / เตือนภัย 33–41.9 / อันตราย
  42–51.9 / อันตรายมาก ≥52 °C) และคำแนะนำการดื่มน้ำของ AAP
- **ข้อจำกัดของการวิจัยครั้งนี้:** เครือข่ายของ environment บล็อกการเปิดหน้าเว็บต้นทางทุกแห่ง
  (fifa.com, pubmed, bmj ฯลฯ) ข้อมูลทั้งหมดได้จากข้อความในดัชนีค้นหาของหน้าที่อ้างอิง ไม่ได้อ่านเต็มหน้า
  จึงต้องมีคนเปิดลิงก์ตรวจซ้ำก่อนใช้จริง (ดู "Open questions")

## 1. Programmes and their terms

### 1a. FIFA 11+ (players aged 14+)

| | |
|---|---|
| What | FIFA's injury-prevention warm-up, developed by F-MARC (FIFA's medical research centre) with an international expert group. [f-marc FIFA 11+](https://www.f-marc.com/fifa-11/) |
| Ages | Amateur players **aged 14 or older**. [f-marc 11plus](http://www.f-marc.com/11plus/) |
| Structure | **3 parts, 15 exercises**, done in order at the start of each session. Part 1: slow running with active stretching and controlled partner contact. Part 2: six exercise sets for core and leg strength, balance and plyometrics/agility, **each with 3 levels** of increasing difficulty. Part 3: moderate/high-speed running with planting and cutting. Before matches only the running parts (1 and 3). [f-marc 11plus](http://www.f-marc.com/11plus/), [f-marc FIFA 11+](https://www.f-marc.com/fifa-11/) |
| Exercise list | Part 1: running straight ahead, hip out, hip in, circling partner, shoulder contact, quick forwards and backwards. Part 2: bench (front plank), sideways bench, hamstrings (Nordic), single-leg stance, squats, jumping; e.g. planks 3 × 20–30 s, squats/jumps 2 × 30 s. Part 3: running across the pitch, bounding, plant and cut. Approximately 8 + 10 + 2 minutes. [Bizzini & Dvorak review, PMC5704377](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5704377/) |
| Dose | **At least twice a week**, about **20 minutes**. [f-marc 11plus](http://www.f-marc.com/11plus/) |
| Evidence | Cluster RCT, 1,892 young female players (13–17), 8-month season: lower risk of injuries overall (rate ratio **0.68**, 95% CI 0.48–0.98), overuse injuries (**0.47**, 0.26–0.85) and severe injuries (**0.55**, 0.36–0.83). Soligard et al., BMJ 2008;337:a2469. [PMC2600961](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2600961/), [PubMed 19066253](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19066253/). Meta-analysis of 4 FIFA 11+ studies: incidence rate ratio **0.61** (0.48–0.77), i.e. **39% fewer injuries**. Thorborg et al., BJSM 2017;51:562. [PubMed 28087568](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28087568/). FIFA's own summary: teams doing it at least twice a week had "30–50% less injured players". [f-marc 11plus](http://www.f-marc.com/11plus/) |

[Inside FIFA terms of service](https://inside.fifa.com/terms-of-service). Permission requests for FIFA IP go through FIFA's mark request process. [FIFA archives user guide](https://www.archives.fifa.com/user_guide) |

### 1b. FIFA 11+ Kids (ages 7–13)

| | |
|---|---|
| What | A children's version of 11+ designed by F-MARC for **7–13-year-olds**, aimed at attention, coordination, balance, lower-limb and core strength, and **falling technique**; no equipment needed. [Meta-analysis, PMC9566496](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9566496/) |
| Structure | **15 minutes, 7 exercises**: a running game, two jumping exercises, a balance/coordination task, two body-stability exercises and a falling technique exercise. [Rössler 2016, PubMed 26508531](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26508531/). Names as reported: jog and look at the coach ("alertness" running game), skating hop, one-leg stance, push-up, one-leg hops, spiderman, roll over. [Systematic review, PMC11264619](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11264619/). **5 difficulty levels per exercise**, moving up only when done correctly several times with few corrections **(secondary: a physiotherapy clinic page, [Axis Sports Medicine](https://www.axissportsmedicine.co.nz/blog/helping-our-kids-warm-up-effectively-the-11-kids); not confirmed on a FIFA page)**. |
| Dose | **At least once a week** to get a preventive effect; **twice a week** recommended. [Rössler 2018, Sports Med](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-017-0834-8) |
| Evidence | Multinational cluster RCT (Switzerland, Germany, Czech Republic, Netherlands; one season): overall injuries **−48%** (HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.32–0.86), severe **−74%** (HR 0.26, 0.10–0.64), lower-extremity **−55%** (HR 0.45, 0.24–0.84); injury rate fell as compliance rose. [Rössler 2018, Sports Med](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-017-0834-8), [PMC5948238](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5948238/). Also improved agility, dynamic balance, slightly jumping and slalom dribbling in 7–12-year-olds. [PubMed 26508531](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26508531/) |
| Terms | Same FIFA site terms as 11+ (above). Training material for it is offered through FIFA Medical Network courses behind a free account. [FIFA Medical Network injury prevention](https://www.fifamedicalnetwork.com/courses/injury-prevention/), [register](https://www.fifamedicalnetwork.com/register/) |

**We MAY / MAY NOT**: same as 11+. Especially: describe the seven movement ideas in our own

## 2. Age-band guidance

### Applies to both bands

- **Rest and volume** (AAP): at least **1–2 days off per week** from the main sport; at least
  **3 months off a year** in 1-month blocks (other activity is fine); delay specialisation until
  about **15–16**. [Brenner 2016, Pediatrics](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/138/3/e20162148/52612/Sports-Specialization-and-Intensive-Training-in),
  [HealthyChildren summary](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/Preventing-Overuse-Injuries.aspx).
  More organised-sport **hours per week than age in years**, or an organised:free-play ratio
  **> 2:1**, raised overuse-injury risk. [AAP 2024 clinical report](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/153/2/e2023065129/196435/Overuse-Injuries-Overtraining-and-Burnout-in-Young),
  [AAP News](https://publications.aap.org/aapnews/news/14416/Too-much-too-soon-Overtraining-can-lead-to-injury)
- **IOC consensus**: plan "sufficient and regular rest and recovery", watch load, and avoid
  early single-sport specialisation; the growth spurt spans roughly **9–15 years** and growth-plate
  injuries are a particular concern. [Bergeron et al. 2015, BJSM / IOC PDF](https://stillmed.olympics.com/media/Documents/Athletes/Medical-Scientific/Consensus-Statements/2015_youth-athletic-development.pdf),
  [PubMed 26084524](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26084524/)
- **Resistance training** (AAP 2020, reaffirmed 2024): can start when balance and posture
  control allow, about **7–8 years**; **2–3 sessions/week, 20–30 min**, at least 8 weeks for
  gains; begin with **1–2 sets of 8–12 reps at low load (≤60% 1RM)**; qualified supervision with
  real-time feedback. [AAP clinical report](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/145/6/e20201011/76942/Resistance-Training-for-Children-and-Adolescents),
  [HealthyChildren strength training](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/sports/Pages/Strength-Training.aspx).
  NSCA: 2–3×/week on **non-consecutive days**, 1–2 sets at first. [NSCA 2009 position statement](https://www.nsca.com/globalassets/about/position-statements/position_stand_youth_resistance_training---2009.pdf).
  NSCA LTAD (2016): motor skills and muscular strength are core pillars for all youth.
  [NSCA LTAD position](https://journals.lww.com/nsca-jscr/fulltext/2016/06000/national_strength_and_conditioning_association.1.aspx)
- **Heat** (Thailand is hot):
  - Thai Department of Health heat-index levels (2024 notice): watch **27.0–32.9 °C**,
    warning **33.0–41.9**, danger **42.0–51.9** (cut outdoor activity time), extreme danger
    **≥ 52.0**; children are a risk group. [Anamai notice B.E. 2567](https://laws.anamai.moph.go.th/th/practices/219070),
    [TMD heat index](http://www.rnd.tmd.go.th/heatindexanalysis/)
  - AAP: in heat, youth risk comes mainly from undue exertion, too little recovery between bouts,
    and heat-retaining clothing; drink about **100–250 mL every 20 min (ages 9–12)** and up to
    **1.0–1.5 L per hour (adolescents)**, starting well hydrated. [AAP policy, reaffirmed 2025](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/128/3/e741/30624/Climatic-Heat-Stress-and-Exercising-Children-and)
  - Football Australia: 90-s drinks break each half at WBGT 26–27.9 °C (or air ≥ 31 °C); may
    delay or postpone at WBGT ≥ 28 °C. [Football Australia heat policy](https://footballaustralia.com.au/ffa-heat-policy)
- **Growth-related pain**:
  - Osgood-Schlatter: pain and swelling at the bump below the kneecap, worse with running and
    jumping, during growth spurts. [HealthyChildren](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/Knee-Pain-and-Osgood-Schlatter-Disease.aspx)
  - Sever's disease: heel pain in active children about **8–15**, during growth spurts; treated
    by resting from the sport until pain goes. [HealthyChildren](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/Heel-Pain-and-Severs-Disease.aspx),
    [AAOS OrthoInfo](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/diseases--conditions/severs-disease/)
  - Peak height velocity is typically about **11–13 (girls)** and **13–15 (boys)**, a period of higher
    injury risk and temporary loss of coordination ("adolescent awkwardness"). [PMC12196770](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12196770/),
    [PMC6293374](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6293374/)

## 3. How self-directed youth training apps structure programmes (short)

- **Techne Futbol**: weekly new dribbling, passing and juggling training; each session has drills
  with demo video and written description and adjustable difficulty; profile stats for daily and
  weekly time trained and a **training streak**; a "Training Sock" level system unlocked by logged
  time; leaderboards; offline drills. [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1298569303)
- **DribbleUp**: "Training Plans" (formerly programs) to keep to a class schedule; 2,000+ guided
  drills with a smart ball; daily and weekly **streaks** with streak avatars; parent view of
  sessions, drills completed and consistency. [Smart soccer ball](https://dribbleup.com/products/smart-soccer-ball),
  [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dribbleup-sports-fitness/id1451878715)
- **Train Effective**: 150+ drills, sessions, workouts and multi-week programmes; custom plans
  scheduled around the player's goals and available time. [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1425844780)

Common pattern: **programme → weeks → sessions → drills**, a chosen schedule, check-in per
session, streaks and levels. For minors, daily streaks reward volume. Our design should reward
**planned sessions completed with rest days kept**, not consecutive days (AAP rest guidance above).

## 4. Proposed starter programmes (draft, all "awaiting coach review")

Rules for every programme:
- XP is `self`-reported and never feeds Power Rating (AGENTS rule 8).
- The scheduler refuses fewer than 2 rest days/week for U10–U13 and fewer than 1 for U14–U17,
  never puts bodyweight-strength days back to back, and warns when the athlete's declared total
  football hours/week exceed their age.
- Every session opens with a pain check ("knee below kneecap / heel / other pain?"). Yes →
  session not started; suggest rest and telling a parent/coach.
- Heat check: show today's heat-index level (Thai Department of Health bands); at "danger" or above,
  suggest moving to early morning/evening or skipping.
- Drill text is ours. "Basis" names the source of the **idea**; the drill design is ours unless
  it says "adapted from".
